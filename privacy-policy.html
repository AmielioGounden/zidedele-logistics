Privacy Policy.

How Zidedele Logistics Solutions collects, uses and protects personal information.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Zidedele Logistics Solutions respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information submitted through our website and during normal business communications.

Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect personal and business information including:

  • Your full name
  • Company name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Service requirements
  • Preferred service date
  • Collection or origin details
  • Delivery or destination details
  • Cargo or operational requirements
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide to us

We may also receive limited technical information generated when you use our website, such as browser information and information required for website security, operation and spam prevention.

How We Use Your Information

Zidedele Logistics Solutions may use information submitted to us for purposes including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests for quotations
  • Assessing logistics or supply chain requirements
  • Communicating with you about requested services
  • Preparing quotations or service proposals
  • Providing and administering services
  • Maintaining business and operational records
  • Protecting our website and communication systems against misuse, spam or fraudulent activity
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations

Personal information will be used only for legitimate business purposes associated with your enquiry, our services or applicable legal requirements.

Information Submitted Through the Website

Information submitted through the Zidedele Logistics Solutions website contact or quotation form is sent to authorised company personnel for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

Please avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information through the website unless that information is specifically required for the service you are requesting.

Sharing of Personal Information

Zidedele Logistics Solutions does not sell personal information.

Personal information may be shared with authorised employees, contractors, service providers or professional advisers where this is reasonably necessary to provide our services, operate our website, support our systems or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, appropriate measures should be applied to protect that information.

Storage and Retention

Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including operational, contractual, legal, accounting and compliance requirements.

Information that is no longer required should be securely deleted or disposed of where reasonably practicable.

Security

Zidedele Logistics Solutions takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

External Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or embedded services, including mapping services.

These third parties may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms. Zidedele Logistics Solutions is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites or services.

Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use essential browser technologies required for normal website functionality, security and performance.

If additional analytics, advertising or tracking technologies are introduced in future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those services and any additional consent requirements.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable South African data protection legislation, including the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), you may have the right to request access to personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion where legally permitted.

Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal, contractual or record-retention requirements.

Consent

Where you voluntarily submit personal information through our website, you acknowledge that the information may be processed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Where the website requests specific consent, including through an enquiry form consent checkbox, information will only be submitted once the required consent has been provided.

Updates to This Privacy Policy

Zidedele Logistics Solutions may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, legal obligations or information-handling practices.

The most recent version will be published on this website with the applicable updated date.
